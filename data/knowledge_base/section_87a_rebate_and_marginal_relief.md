# Section 87A Tax Rebate and Marginal Relief Computation Guide

**Source Organization:** Central Board of Direct Taxes, Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance  
**Applicable Assessment Years:** AY 2024-25, AY 2025-26, AY 2026-27  
**Document ID:** GOI-CBDT-SEC-87A-2024  
**Source URL:** https://incometaxindia.gov.in/

### 1. Section 87A Rebate Thresholds
The tax rebate under Section 87A provides targeted tax relief for lower and middle-income resident individuals:

| Parameter | Old Tax Regime | New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Taxable Income Threshold** | Up to ₹5,00,000 | Up to ₹7,00,000 |
| **Maximum Rebate Amount** | ₹12,500 (or 100% of income-tax) | ₹25,000 (or 100% of income-tax) |
| **Effective Net Tax Payable** | **₹0** if taxable income <= ₹5,00,000 | **₹0** if taxable income <= ₹7,00,000 |
| **Gross Salary Breakeven (Salaried)** | ₹5,50,000 (with ₹50k std deduction) | **₹7,75,000** (with ₹75k std deduction in FY 24-25) |

### 2. Marginal Relief under Section 87A in New Regime
Under the New Tax Regime, when an individual's taxable income marginally exceeds ₹7,00,000, a sudden tax liability of over ₹20,000 could otherwise arise on earning even ₹1 extra. 
To prevent this anomaly, the Finance Act introduced **Marginal Relief**:
- **Rule:** The tax payable on total income exceeding ₹7,00,000 shall not exceed the amount by which total income exceeds ₹7,00,000.
- **Formula:** 
  $$\text{Tax Payable} = \min(\text{Calculated Slab Tax}, \text{Taxable Income} - ₹7,00,000)$$
- **Effective Range:** Applies for taxable incomes between ₹7,00,001 and ₹7,27,777.
