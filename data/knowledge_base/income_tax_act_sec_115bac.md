# Section 115BAC: Concessional Tax Regime for Individuals and HUF (New Tax Regime)

**Source Organization:** Income Tax Department, Ministry of Finance, Government of India  
**Applicable Assessment Years:** AY 2024-25, AY 2025-26, AY 2026-27  
**Effective Date:** 1st April 2023 (Default Regime from FY 2023-24 onward)  
**Document ID:** GOI-CBDT-SEC-115BAC-2024  
**Source URL:** https://incometaxindia.gov.in/acts/income-tax-act-1961.aspx  

---

### 1. Default Tax Regime Status
Under Section 115BAC(1A), the New Tax Regime is the default tax regime for individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), Association of Persons (AOPs), and Bodies of Individuals (BOIs) starting Assessment Year 2024-25 (Financial Year 2023-24). A taxpayer desiring to be taxed under the Old Tax Regime must exercise an explicit opt-out option before the due date under Section 139(1) for filing the return of income.

---

### 2. Tax Slabs Structure

#### Slabs for FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26) and FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27):
*Amended by Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024:*
- **Up to ₹3,00,000:** Nil (0%)
- **₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000:** 5% of amount exceeding ₹3,00,000
- **₹7,00,001 to ₹10,00,000:** ₹20,000 + 10% of amount exceeding ₹7,00,000
- **₹10,00,001 to ₹12,00,000:** ₹50,000 + 15% of amount exceeding ₹10,00,000
- **₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000:** ₹80,000 + 20% of amount exceeding ₹12,00,000
- **Above ₹15,00,000:** ₹1,40,000 + 30% of amount exceeding ₹15,00,000

#### Slabs for FY 2023-24 (AY 2024-25):
- **Up to ₹3,00,000:** Nil
- **₹3,00,001 to ₹6,00,000:** 5%
- **₹6,00,001 to ₹9,00,000:** 10%
- **₹9,00,001 to ₹12,00,000:** 15%
- **₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000:** 20%
- **Above ₹15,00,000:** 30%

---

### 3. Deductions Allowed and Disallowed in New Regime

#### Allowed Deductions:
1. **Standard Deduction on Salary Income:**
   - FY 2023-24: ₹50,000.
   - FY 2024-25 & FY 2025-26: Increased to **₹75,000** for salaried employees and pensioners under Section 16(ia).
2. **Employer's Contribution to NPS (Section 80CCD(2)):**
   - Allowed up to 14% of salary (Basic + DA) for government employees and private sector employees (enhanced to 14% in Union Budget 2024).
3. **Family Pension Deduction:**
   - ₹25,000 or 1/3rd of pension (increased from ₹15,000 in Budget 2024).

#### Disallowed Deductions:
- Section 80C (Life insurance, PPF, EPF, ELSS, tuition fees, home loan principal).
- Section 80D (Health insurance premiums for self, spouse, children, and parents).
- Section 24(b) (Interest on home loan for self-occupied property).
- Section 10(13A) (House Rent Allowance - HRA).
- Section 10(5) (Leave Travel Concession - LTC).
- Section 80TTA / 80TTB (Interest on savings bank accounts and deposits).

---

### 4. Section 87A Tax Rebate in New Regime
Under Section 87A, a resident individual whose total income does not exceed **₹7,00,000** is entitled to a rebate of 100% of the income-tax payable or **₹25,000**, whichever is less. Consequently, a salaried employee with a gross salary of up to **₹7,75,000** in FY 2024-25 pays **₹0 tax** (₹7,75,000 - ₹75,000 standard deduction = ₹7,00,000 taxable income).
