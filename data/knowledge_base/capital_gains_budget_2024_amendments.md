# Union Budget July 2024 Amendments: Rationalization of Capital Gains Taxation

**Source Organization:** Ministry of Finance, Government of India (Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024)  
**Effective Date:** 23rd July 2024  
**Applicable Assessment Years:** AY 2025-26, AY 2026-27  
**Document ID:** GOI-MOF-BUDGET-2024-CAPGAINS  
**Source URL:** https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/  

---

### 1. Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) under Section 111A
- **Applicable Assets:** Listed equity shares, units of equity-oriented mutual funds, and business trusts where Securities Transaction Tax (STT) is paid.
- **Previous Tax Rate (prior to 23 July 2024):** 15%.
- **Amended Tax Rate (on or after 23 July 2024):** **20%** (plus applicable surcharge and 4% cess).
- **Other Short-Term Capital Gains:** Unlisted assets and debt mutual funds bought after 31 March 2023 continue to be taxed at individual applicable slab rates.

---

### 2. Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) under Section 112A
- **Holding Period for Classification as Long-Term:**
  - Listed equity and equity mutual fund units: more than 12 months.
  - Listed bonds, debentures, unlisted units: more than 12 months.
  - Real estate (immovable property) and unlisted shares: more than 24 months.
- **Previous Tax Rate:** 10% on gains exceeding ₹1,00,000 per financial year.
- **Amended Tax Rate (effective 23 July 2024):** **12.5%** across all asset classes.
- **Enhanced Exemption Limit:** The annual exemption threshold under Section 112A has been increased from ₹1,00,000 to **₹1,25,000** for FY 2024-25 and subsequent years.

---

### 3. Real Estate and Immovable Property LTCG
- **Rate:** 12.5% without indexation benefit for sales executed on or after 23 July 2024.
- **Grandfathering Protection:** For properties acquired prior to 23 July 2024 by resident individuals and HUFs, taxpayers have the option to compute tax either at **12.5% without indexation** or at **20% with indexation**, whichever is lower.
