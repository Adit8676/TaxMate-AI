# Understanding Form 16: Structure, Components, and Field Mapping

**Source Organization:** Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Income Tax Department  
**Applicable Assessment Years:** AY 2024-25, AY 2025-26, AY 2026-27  
**Document ID:** GOI-CBDT-FORM16-GUIDE-2024  
**Source URL:** https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/  

---

### 1. Overview of Form 16
Form 16 is the certificate of deduction of tax at source (TDS) issued under Section 203 of the Income Tax Act, 1961 by an employer to an employee. It consists of two essential parts:
- **Part A:** Generated via the TRACES portal containing Employer TAN, Employee PAN, quarterly summary of TDS deducted and deposited into the Central Government account.
- **Part B:** Prepared by the employer containing a comprehensive breakdown of salary paid, exempt allowances, deductions claimed under Chapter VI-A, total taxable income, and net tax payable or refund due.

---

### 2. Standard Part B Line-Item Mapping
1. **Gross Salary (Item 1):**
   - **1(a) Salary as per provisions contained in sec. 17(1):** Basic salary, DA, HRA, bonuses, commissions, and special allowances.
   - **1(b) Value of perquisites under sec. 17(2):** Company car, accommodation, ESOP perquisites.
   - **1(c) Profits in lieu of salary under sec. 17(3):** Severance, retrenchment compensation.
   - **Total Gross Salary:** Sum of 1(a) + 1(b) + 1(c).

2. **Allowances exempt under Section 10 (Item 2):**
   - Travel allowance, Leave Travel Assistance (Sec 10(5)).
   - House Rent Allowance exempt under Sec 10(13A).

3. **Deductions under Section 16 (Item 4):**
   - **4(a) Standard deduction under sec. 16(ia):** ₹50,000 (Old Regime) or ₹75,000 (New Regime for FY 24-25).
   - **4(b) Entertainment allowance under sec. 16(ii):** (Govt employees only).
   - **4(c) Tax on employment / Professional Tax under sec. 16(iii):** Usually up to ₹2,500 annually.

4. **Income chargeable under the head "Salaries" (Item 6):**
   - Gross salary minus exempt allowances minus Section 16 deductions.

5. **Deductions under Chapter VI-A (Item 9 & 10):**
   - **Section 80C:** EPF, PPF, Life Insurance, ELSS (max ₹1.5L).
   - **Section 80D:** Mediclaim for self and parents.
   - **Section 80CCD(1B):** Voluntary NPS up to ₹50,000.
   - **Section 80CCD(2):** Employer contribution to NPS.

6. **Total Taxable Income (Item 12):**
   - Net income after subtracting Chapter VI-A deductions.
7. **Total Tax Payable (Item 16) and Net Tax Payable / Refund (Item 18):**
   - Tax on total income plus surcharge and 4% Health & Education cess minus TDS deducted.
